PLUTO HIGH-RESOLUTION MONOCHROME DETAIL
New Horizons Team; NASA, Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory, Southwest Research Institute, Lunar and Planetary Institute.
Publisher: USGS Astrogeology Science Center.
Source: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/pluto_new_horizons_lorri_mvic_global_mosaic_300m
Access constraints: None. Use constraints: Please cite authors.
Dataset: Pluto New Horizons LORRI - MVIC Global Mosaic 300 m, July 14, 2017.
Native raster: 24888 x 12444 pixels; simple cylindrical/equirectangular projection; east-positive longitude 0 to 360 degrees; latitude -90 to 90 degrees.
The source images have varying observed detail across the globe and unmapped areas remain present.

Imported through USGS WMS as two original 4096 x 4096 JPEG responses, downsampled by the provider from the native raster. No local image edits or upscaling were applied.
pluto-detail-west-4k.jpg: longitude 0 to 180 degrees east; latitude -90 to 90 degrees; image top = 90 N and bottom = 90 S.
pluto-detail-east-4k.jpg: longitude 180 to 360 degrees east; latitude -90 to 90 degrees; image top = 90 N and bottom = 90 S.
Together these provide 8192 x 4096 equivalent global sampling. Shader color tint from the separate NASA preview is a visualization treatment, not an additional observed color image.
Reference: Schenk et al. (2018), Basin, Fractures and Volcanoes: Global Cartography and Topography of Pluto from New Horizons, Icarus 314, 400-433. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2018.06.008

RESOLUTION, COVERAGE AND VISUAL EFFECTS
Native source dimensions are recorded in the asset manifest. Lighting, atmosphere and procedural relief overlays are artistic effects, not additional observations or authentic interior maps.
The NASA and USGS Pluto maps contain black regions near the south pole where surface data are unavailable. Any visual fill in those areas is illustrative rather than observed terrain.
